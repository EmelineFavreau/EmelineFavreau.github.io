Computational Biologist at University of Cambridge with [Prof Eoin McKinney](https://www.immunology.cam.ac.uk/Networkdirectory/eoin), working on Chronic Kidney Disease within a GSK-Cambridge collaboration called [CG-TIC](https://www.med.cam.ac.uk/cg-tic). Currently wrangling EHR data.

Until recently, I wrote pipelines to handle rare disease molecular data and produce reports for clinicians (see my [CV](https://emelinefavreau.github.io/cv/)). I [blogged](https://emelinefavreau.github.io/blog/) about my previous work with animal genomes, transcriptomes, and TAD data. 

I provide multi-omics analytical research of a large, dynamic group between academia and industry. I have a track record of improving both research findings and technical operations, from foundational data analyses to advanced pipeline development and interpretation.

Want to know more? Reach out to [me](https://emelinefavreau.github.io/contact/) and read some [papers](https://emelinefavreau.github.io/publications/).

The neurodegenerative disease of my niece has driven me to shift my path from insect evolutionary biology to disease omics. I keep an eye on Batten disease literature, [see our French outreach website on CLN3 disease](http://cln.jmfavreau.info/index.html).
